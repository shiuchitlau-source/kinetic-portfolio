# Prototype Instructions

Run the local server yourself and open the preview in the browser available to this environment. Do not give the user server-start instructions when you can run it.

Before making substantial visual changes, use the Product Design plugin's `get-context` skill when the visual source is unclear or no longer matches the current goal. When the user gives durable prototype-specific design feedback, preferences, or decisions, record them in `AGENTS.md`.

When implementing from a selected generated mock, treat that image as the source of truth for layout, component anatomy, density, spacing, color, typography, visible content, and hierarchy.

The approved portfolio direction is the quieter “Silent Nameplate” polish: use a compact two-line intro, oversized ivory nameplate hero, sharp white-to-black transition, and an immediate two-project reveal with cinematic negative space. Preserve sparse corner navigation, restrained orange accents, real project content, hover previews, case-study drawers, and YouTube playback.

Use `/assets/b-logo-angular-option-2.png` consistently for the header monogram, browser favicon, and Apple touch icon.

The current title-section direction refines that nameplate into an editorial masthead: use a compact monogram/navigation/availability rail, a layered orange halftone horse herd with cobalt-blue shadow pixels, and a continuous “MAKE PIXELS MOVE” marquee above the work transition. Keep the darkest horse shade blue (`#1746d1`) while preserving the remaining orange palette. Render every horse as code-driven canvas pixels from the normalized coordinate data in `src/horseFrames.js`; do not restore GIF, video, or raster sprite playback. Give every horse a fully opaque paper-coloured silhouette beneath its coloured dots. Preserve visual depth through horse size and dot intensity: the lighter small horses must remain behind the marquee, while the stronger foreground horses sit above it and prevent letters showing through their silhouettes. Keep the marquee as a true `100vw` full-bleed layer at every responsive breakpoint, independent of the padded hero content width. Each continuous marquee loop must explicitly begin with “MAKE” aligned to the left edge and move without gaps or sudden pops; keep enough duplicated copy that the track always fills the viewport so the seam stays invisible. Keep every horse anchored to a fixed location with a slow, subtle independent wiggle like the original single-horse drift; do not make the horses travel across the screen. Vary their sizes, but keep the entire herd smaller than the former dominant single horse. The herd itself is the only motion control: a click anywhere on the scene pauses the frame cycle and every wiggle, but the marquee keeps rolling; the next click resumes horse motion with equivalent keyboard behavior. Do not restore a separate motion switch. Reduced-motion mode shows a stable layered composition. Do not restore “Designing Bold Motion Experiences,” “MOTION,” another static display headline, or the removed “Motion for” discipline rail in this title slot. On mobile, keep the herd visible without clipping, reserve a clear text-only band above every horse frame for the “Independent motion designer” kicker, and replace the desktop links with the fullscreen Menu trigger.

On desktop landscape viewports, the entire hero should occupy the first screen at `100svh`: keep the floating navigation near the top, scale the horse from both available width and height so the full silhouette remains visible, and hold the discipline rail at the bottom of the viewport. Do not force that landscape sizing onto tablet or mobile portrait layouts.

The header identity uses the selected orange angular “B” monogram in `public/assets/b-logo-angular-option-2.png`, paired with the exact name “Benjamin Lau” as a compact two-line black/grey lockup. Preserve its full-height stem, rounded bowls, and rising diagonal bridge. Do not restore the L or forward slash, and do not replace the mark with a typed initial.

Use Apple-style interaction polish as a craft layer rather than a new visual theme: preserve the editorial layout and sharp media frames, keep glass only for floating functional chrome, provide immediate press feedback and at least 44px touch targets, make overlays enter and leave along the same path, and support reduced motion, reduced transparency, and increased contrast. Do not convert the page into rounded card grids or a generic Apple product-page imitation.

Within the black work section, use large editorial project frames rather than a compact constellation. Wise should dominate the upper right, Samsung should form a substantial second beat on the left, and later projects should continue in a widely spaced staggered sequence. Keep scroll-driven scale motion on the frames.

Use Wise Future Store and Samsung Skate Park as the two canonical project-title patterns. On desktop, right-aligned imagery uses the Wise pattern with its label on the left, while left-aligned imagery uses the Samsung pattern with its label on the right. Keep each label optically close to its image rather than floating at the outer page edge. The visible title-to-image gap must be identical across every project even while scroll scaling is active; anchor scaling to the title-facing edge of each image. Keep the project number, title, category, and year typography identical across every item. At tablet and mobile widths, every title stacks closely below its image and aligns to the image’s leading edge, with scaling anchored to the image’s bottom edge. Project frames and their labels must never overlap at any responsive width, including while scroll-driven scaling is active. Preserve the staggered look with normal flow and reserved vertical gaps rather than fixed absolute top positions.

Project subpages should use a compact introduction and metadata rail followed by a dominant full-width lead image. Arrange the first two supporting images in an asymmetric 42/58 split, let later media return to full width, and keep previous/next navigation balanced at the bottom. When a project has a YouTube film, the privacy-enhanced player occupies the same lead-media slot. Collapse the gallery to a single column on mobile.

Build app UI in `src/`. Keep `.openai/hosting.json`, `worker/index.js`, `scripts/prepare-sites-build.mjs`, and `tests/sites-worker.test.mjs` intact so the same local prototype can be handed to Sites. Before a Sites handoff, run `npm run build` and `npm run test:sites`; the build must leave `dist/client/index.html`, `dist/server/index.js`, and `dist/.openai/hosting.json`.
